[h1]Traditional Preservation (Salt)[/h1]

Make your own salt from the land. Find a natural salt lick, dig up the saline soil and leach it into brine in a wooden vat, or draw weak brine from its springs, and boil the brine down over a fire.

Salt is one of the few things you can't grow or raise in Project Zomboid. This mod gives long-term survivors a slow, hands-on way to make it, based on how people really got salt from salty ground.

[b]Build 42 only (42.22 or newer). This is an early release, so back up your save before enabling it. Singleplayer only: multiplayer is not supported.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]15 natural salt licks across the map, with one within reach of every town
[*]Each save places its licks differently, so you have to go looking
[*]Licks of dark, salt-flecked soil beside brine springs, as at Kentucky's real licks
[*]A full process from dirt to salt: dig, leach, boil, scrape; or fill a bucket at a spring and boil it
[*]A buildable Leaching Vat with its own art, two facings, and visible soil and bucket while it works
[*]Salt licks regrow slowly, tile by tile, so they last for the whole run
[*]Evaporated Salt works as seasoning with no other mods, and has its own 3D model
[*]Bags and boxes for storing salt, which also save weight
[*]An optional bridge mod for Long Term Preservation Extended
[/list]

[h2]How it works[/h2]

[h3]1. Find a salt lick[/h3]
Salt licks are 3 to 7 tiles joined edge to edge on natural ground: dark mineral soil flecked with white, and brine springs, shallow blue-grey pools rimmed with salt. A lick of 3 or 4 tiles has 1 soil tile, one of 5 to 7 has 2. There are 15 in the world, one per region, in woodland, most of them near water. Every town has one within about 1,500 tiles, so it takes a bit of searching but stays realistic. Each region is 80 by 80 tiles, and every save picks its own spot inside it, which then stays put for that whole save.

[h3]2. Dig Saline Soil[/h3]
Right-click a deposit tile with a shovel and an empty sack in your inventory. Digging takes 3 in-game minutes, uses up the sack and gives a 5 kg Sack of Saline Soil.

Each tile holds 5 diggings and regrows one a week on its own clock, even while you're away. A tile with nothing left turns paler and loses most of its white flecks until it regrows.

[h3]Or fill at a spring[/h3]
Right-click a spring and choose Fill with Spring Brine: no tools, about a minute. It fills the container in your hand, else a metal bucket, else any empty container. Spring brine is weak: a full bucket is 1 L of Brine and 9 L of tainted water, and boils to 1 salt. Boil it as it is, or use it in place of water in a Leaching Charge for one salt more. A spring holds 30 L and fills back 10 L a day, even while you're away; drawn dry, it shows mud until it refills.

[h3]3. Prepare a Leaching Charge[/h3]
Combine a Sack of Saline Soil with a full metal bucket of water (clean or tainted) or brine. You get your sack back.

[h3]4. Build a Leaching Vat[/h3]
Requires Carpentry 3. It takes 6 planks, 12 nails, a cloth filter, a hammer and a saw (both kept).

It can only be built outdoors, not under a roof. The narrow V-shaped end is the front, and the vat has two facings. You can choose one when building, and an empty vat can be picked up, moved and rotated. A vat that's holding something or running can't be moved.

[h3]5. Leach Brine[/h3]
Load the charge into the vat and start it. After 2 in-game hours you take out a bucket of brine: the soil adds 5 L of Brine to what went in (water gives 5, spring brine 6). Use a full bucket of brine as the next charge's liquid and it comes out stronger, though strong brine takes up less: water goes 5, then 8, then 10 L of Brine. The clock keeps running while you're away. The vat shows the soil and a bucket under the keel as it works, then spent soil and a full bucket until you take the bucket out.

[h3]6. Boil the Brine[/h3]
Put the bucket of brine into a lit fire, stove or oven: it becomes a Bucket of Boiling Brine, and a part-used bucket boils too. Taken off before it is done, it is a bucket of brine again. Hover a bucket to see its strength and the salt it boils to. Scraping gives 1 Evaporated Salt per litre of Brine. It takes about 4 in-game hours on a fire, or about 2 in an oven turned all the way up, and burns fuel as normal. It turns into a Bucket of Salt Crust.

[h3]7. Scrape the Salt Crust[/h3]
Scraping needs a knife or meat cleaver, which you keep but which can wear slightly. It gives 1 Evaporated Salt per litre of Brine (5 from one pass of water) and the bucket you used, plain or forged.

[h2]Using your salt[/h2]
Evaporated Salt counts as salt in any recipe that takes salt, and seasons soups and stews.

[h3]Packaging[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Make Bag of Salt:[/b] 10 Evaporated Salt + an empty paper bag. The bag holds all the salt from those 10 portions (50 uses) and counts as salt itself, so you can season and cook straight from it. Bags can't be unpacked, but they refill shakers, and a used-up bag leaves its paper bag behind.
[*][b]Pack Box of Salt Bags:[/b] 5 full bags make 1 box. A sealed box doesn't count as salt, so unpack it first.
[*][b]Unpack Box of Salt Bags:[/b] gives back 5 bags. You can also double-click the box.
[/list]

Plain, Jays and Spiffo's paper bags all work. Only full salt and full bags can be packed, so packing can't turn a part-used one back into a full one.

Packaging also saves weight. 50 salt weighs 5.0 loose, 4.0 in bags, and 3.5 in a box.

[h2]Refilling salt shakers[/h2]
Vanilla bread, pizza, pie dough, biscuits, guacamole and jarring only accept a vanilla salt shaker. A used-up shaker now leaves a Shaker (Empty) behind, and [b]Refill Salt Shaker with Evaporated Salt[/b] turns it and 2 full Evaporated Salt into a real vanilla shaker that works in all of them. [b]Refill Salt Shaker from a Bag of Salt[/b] does the same with 10 of the bag's 50 uses.

[h2]Long Term Preservation Extended[/h2]
Traditional Preservation (Salt) requires [b]Traditional Preservation (Core)[/b]: subscribe to it too. If you use Long Term Preservation Extended, also subscribe to [b]Traditional Preservation (Salt) - LTP Bridge[/b]. It requires both mods and adds:
[list]
[*][b]Make Jar of Evaporated Salt:[/b] jar, lid, label, a writing tool (kept) and 2 Evaporated Salt
[*][b]Pack Salt Sack:[/b] 50 evaporated or crushed salt + an empty sack
[*][b]Make Bag of Salt (Mixed):[/b] 10 evaporated or crushed salt in any mix + an empty paper bag
[/list]

The jar recipe matches LTP's own amounts. All three refuse part-used Evaporated Salt, and Pack Salt Sack also refuses a sack with items in it. There is no direct recipe from Evaporated Salt to crushed salt, but LTP's unpack recipes still give crushed salt from jars and sacks.

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Salt lick regrowth (days)[/b] in the sandbox options sets how long a soil tile takes to regrow one digging (7 by default), and a spring's refill with it (10 L a day at 7).
[*]Hovering Dig Saline Soil shows how many diggings the spot has left, and Fill with Spring Brine how many litres the spring holds.
[*]The mod adds no loot, and doesn't change any vanilla or LTP recipe or loot table.
[*]Salt licks appear the first time their area loads, so you can add the mod to an existing save. Regions added in later versions reach existing saves too.
[*]The tiledef number 5171 must be unique among your active mods.
[/list]
